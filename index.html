Pangenome Data Catalog

Which sequencing and functional assays exist for which donors across the two major human pangenome cohorts — HPRC, and HGSVC phases 2 and 3 — and what other projects have measured on the same cell lines. Built by enumerating HPRC's S3 bucket and the IGSR archive directly, and by reading the first few hundred KB of the BAMs to see which already carry methylation calls.

        Before you trust this — caveats that change what the numbers mean
        • Methylation is usually already in the reads. PacBio Revio/jasmine and ONT dorado BAMs carry 5mC (ONT also 5hmC) as MM/ML tags. HiFi 5mC and ONT 5mC mean those tags were found in the first ~1,000 reads of a probed BAM, or that a dedicated methylation submission exists. A donor without the flag may still have it in BAMs that were not probed; FASTQ-only data never does.
        • Index tables are not the archive. HPRC's GitHub index tables cover only part of the bucket, and Fiber-seq, Pore-C, Micro-C, direct RNA, Bionano, Strand-seq and the Coriell karyotypes/arrays have no index at all. Unindexed is a provenance note, not a quality signal.
        • submissions/ holds data working/ does not. Ten donors (the HG084xx whole-blood vs LCL pilot) have no working/ directory at all. Every raw-data submission is classified by name; files already promoted to working/ are not counted twice.
        • HGSVC "sheet only" means you cannot download it from the HGSVC directories. For Illumina, most of those donors are in the 1000 Genomes NYGC 30x set on the same archive, shown as nygc_30x.
        • HGSVC2 and HGSVC3 are phases of one cohort, not separate cohorts: every donor in the HGSVC2 indexes is also on the HGSVC production sheet. Each donor shows which IGSR collection holds its files.
        • Other-project data is matched on the cell-line id. Geuvadis, MAGE, ENCODE and 1KG-ONT sequenced the same Coriell lines, but a different culture and passage. Treat them as the same genome, not the same cells.
        • Trio parents' reads are not the sample's. HPRC stores parental Illumina under the child; it is shown as its own assay.
        • Remaining unattributed files are mostly pooled Strand-seq flowcells and multiplexed Kinnex pools with no per-sample name. They, the 1000G trio parents and the Coriell lines screened but never sequenced are kept with their URLs in _unattributed.json.
        • ONT 6mA chromatin stencilling is not a separate assay. It is a basecall variant inside the UCSC ONT Fiber-seq submission, shown as a file detail.
        • Iso-Seq, PacBio CLR, 10x and microarrays are legacy, shown in italics.

        Sources: human-pangenomics · IGSR / HGSVC2 · HGSVC3 · 1kGP NYGC 30x · Geuvadis · MAGE · ENCODE. Underlying data is openly licensed by the respective consortia; this catalogue only indexes it. Regenerate with scripts/build_catalog.py.
