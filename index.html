        Before you trust this — caveats that change what the numbers mean
        • Index tables are not the archive. HPRC's GitHub index tables resolve only about 9,700 of 19,807 files, and eight modalities have no index at all. An unindexed file is normal data that postdates the last index refresh — it is a provenance note, not a quality signal.
        • HGSVC3 "sheet only" means you cannot download it. The production spreadsheet asserts Illumina WGS for all 69 samples, but only 6 have files on IGSR; the rest live at the Broad. Every HGSVC3 assay here carries its evidence source.
        • A zero means the consortium did not run that assay. HPRC has no short-read RNA-seq; HGSVC3 has no Fiber-seq, HiFi 5mC or Micro-C. Neither is missing data.
        • Multi-modal work is a ~100-sample study, not a 400-sample one. 346 HPRC samples carry the core five, but only 100 add Fiber-seq and only 44 also have HiFi 5mC.
        • 6,143 files cannot be tied to a sample — overwhelmingly pooled or multiplexed Strand-seq libraries. They are real data; they simply have no per-sample attribution. A further 20 name donors outside the catalogued cohorts (including NA24385, GIAB's HG002).
        • ONT 6mA chromatin stencilling is not a separate assay. It is a basecall variant inside the UCSC ONT Fiber-seq submission, flagged on the samples that have it rather than counted as its own modality.
        • Iso-Seq and PacBio CLR are legacy, superseded by Kinnex and HiFi respectively. They are shown in italics and folded into Kinnex_IsoSeq in the unified vocabulary.
